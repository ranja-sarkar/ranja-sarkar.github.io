Databases come in various forms for example, key-value (dictionary) store, document store, graph database, columnar store. Vector database is one form that saves information as multidimensional vectors, an organized store of vector embeddings of data. 

A vector database is one that indexes and stores vector embeddings for fast retrieval of data by similarity search or vector search. Vector databases like pinecone and weaviate are commercially available, while popular open-source ones are chroma and qdrant. Elastic search is one widely deployed vector database, and lanceDB is open-source but offers commercial products with additional features and managed services.

---

In vector databases, vector similarity is used as discussed above however, data stored in graph databases is better suited to reduce hallucinations and answer complex queries with more relevance and accuracy, due to their ability to provide the complex relationships between data objects.
