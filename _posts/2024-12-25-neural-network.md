The earliest neural networks are the feed-forward neural networks (NNs) or multilayer perceptrons (MLPs), where input values flow forward linearly (forward pass) and gradients/derivatives flow backward linearly (backpropagation) through a network. Forward propagation is when data moves from left to right in the network, and backward propagation is when the gradient moves from right (output layer) to left (input layer) in the network.
